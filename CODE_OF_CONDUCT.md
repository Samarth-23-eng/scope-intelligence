# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Scope Intelligence contributors, maintainers, and community members are
committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free environment for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Share context and evidence; assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Respect privacy, source policies, and the sensitive nature of OSINT work.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help newcomers participate safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unlawful surveillance, access-control bypass, credential theft,
  doxxing, or targeted harm.
- Submitting secrets, private datasets, or sensitive investigation material to
  public issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Sustained disruption of project spaces or bad-faith manipulation of evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Serious or
private reports should be sent through GitHub's private repository reporting
features or directly to the repository owner; do not post sensitive evidence
publicly.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
